Featured Project: Frontier

AI-Assisted Medical Claims Review

Frontier is a Human-Computer Interaction capstone project designed to help medical claims reviewers identify billing discrepancies and make informed decisions while maintaining human oversight.

The application uses Python, FastAPI, React, TypeScript, and database technologies.

My research evaluates review time, perceived workload, and accuracy when comparing manual claims review with Frontier-assisted review.
